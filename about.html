/About _

I’m Laszlo, a sound professional based in Bristol, UK. I graduated with First-Class Honours from Bath Spa University with a degree in Creative Music Technology (Sound for Games and Interactive Media), and I’m currently undertaking an MFA in Sound Production for Film and Video Games with ThinkSpace Education and AUB.

I’ve been passionate about music from an early age, which led me to develop skills in arrangement, production and audio engineering. Those same skills naturally extended into my other passions: film and video games, where sound can be just as important in shaping an experience and telling a story.

My work spans sound recording, sound editing, sound design, mixing, music production and arrangement, with a particular interest in creating sound that supports the character and intent of a project rather than simply sitting alongside it. I enjoy working across both the creative and technical sides of audio, from detailed editing and sound design through to mixing and implementation.

I’m particularly interested in opportunities within film, television, games and post-production, and I’m always looking to expand my experience and work alongside people who care about making great things.

Outside of audio, I’m a musician and guitarist, and I’ve always had a fascination with the technology behind the things I use — from recording equipment and synthesizers to electronics and audio systems.
